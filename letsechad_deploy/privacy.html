Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

L'ets Echad ("we," "us," or "our") operates letsechad.com. This page says plainly what information moves through this site, who handles it, and what your choices are.

What We Collect, and Who Processes It
When you subscribe to teaching updates or the free course, you give us your email address. That address is stored and processed by MailerLite, the email service that sends our messages. When you give through the support page, Donorbox processes the gift. We never see or store your card number. Donorbox handles the payment and sends you a receipt. The site itself is served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs, and our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests those font files from Google when a page loads.

What We Do With It
Your email address is used to send the teachings and course emails you signed up for, and nothing else. We do not sell it, trade it, or share it beyond the processors named above. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops all future sends.

Retention and Deletion
We keep your email address while you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, sends stop immediately. If you want your address removed entirely, write to us and we will delete it from MailerLite.

Cookies and Analytics
We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We may review aggregate, non-identifying traffic statistics from our hosting provider to understand which teachings are being read.
