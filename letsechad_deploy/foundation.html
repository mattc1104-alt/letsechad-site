Where to Begin

Something brought you here. Maybe it was a verse that wouldn't let you go. Maybe it was a pull toward Torah you couldn't explain. Maybe you've been asking questions your old framework couldn't answer.

Before we can test any doctrine, we have to examine the way we were taught to read. Most people come to the Bible carrying assumptions they didn't choose: categories, definitions, and instincts inherited from a long chain of interpretation. That isn't a flaw. It is the starting point.

The Bible is not a Western theology manual. It is a high-context, covenantal, Israel-centered collection of writings, rooted in Torah, Temple, priesthood, land, exile, and restoration. Reading it on its own terms changes everything.

The Foundation is a free 5-day email course. One idea a day. One small practice you can do the same afternoon. Not to tear anything down. Not to win arguments. To read honestly.

What You Will Receive

Five Days. Five Shifts.

Day 1

You Are Not a Neutral Reader

Every reader carries assumptions they didn't choose. We start there, not to accuse, but to name what is already shaping what we see. Honest reading begins with honest self-examination.

Day 2

The Bible Was Written for People Who Already Knew the Story

The Bible is a high-context text. Much of its meaning is assumed, carried by shared memory, covenant history, land, priesthood, and exile. A low-context reader misses what was never written down, because it didn't need to be.

Day 3

The Questions the Bible Is Actually Asking

Tanakh and Greek-influenced theology are asking different questions. Who is HaShem? Who are His people? What does holiness require? How does exile end? When you learn what the text is asking, what it is answering becomes clearer.

Day 4

What Translation Costs You

Three Hebrew words English flattens, and what you recover when you open them back up. Teshuvah is not merely remorse. Korban is not merely sacrifice. Shem is not merely a name. Translation is always a loss. This day shows you how to begin recovering it.

Day 5

Where This Leads

The foundation is laid. Day 5 points forward: to the next questions, the next texts, and the next step for anyone who wants to keep building. One stick in His hand. Ezekiel 37:19.
