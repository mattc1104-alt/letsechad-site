Sustain the Work

The teachings are free.
The work is not.

Every dvar Torah, public study guide, and ministry resource on this site is free, and stays free. No one should be prevented from beginning the journey because they cannot pay.

The courses, books, and complete curricula offer something more structured: carefully developed pathways for those who want to study more deeply. Their purchase supports the time, production, and ongoing work required to keep the public teachings freely available.

There are two ways to sustain that work: enroll in a course or give directly. Either way, the work moves forward.

The Book

Para: Uncovered

There is a Hebrew word for what happened at the golden calf: para. Uncovered. Exposed. Stripped of the covenant covering meant to protect Israel. Drawing from Exodus, Hosea, Jeremiah, and the Yom Kippur liturgy, Para: Uncovered traces the danger of substitutes and HaShem’s enduring call: Come back. I will cover you again.

Give Directly

Share in the Work of Reunification

L'ets Echad is a teaching ministry. Its public teachings, study guides, and foundational resources are freely available without a paywall. Paid courses and curricula provide structured, extended instruction while helping sustain the free work of the ministry. The mission stays the same: Torah teaching accessible to anyone sensing the call of return, regardless of where they are financially.

Your support makes that possible. It funds new teaching preparation, study materials, conference participation, and the ongoing work of getting this content in front of people who need it.

We believe that Ephraim's return is a prophetic work, and that work is happening now. Every gift puts a hand to what HaShem is doing in this generation.

Your gift supports

→ Written study guides and downloadable resources

→ Conference teaching participation

→ Website, hosting, and content infrastructure
