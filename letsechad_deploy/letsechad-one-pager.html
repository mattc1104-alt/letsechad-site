One Stick in His Hand

“Behold, I am taking the stick of Joseph, which is in the hand of Ephraim, together with the tribes of Israel his companions, and I will place them upon the stick of Judah. I will make them into one stick, and
they will be one in My hand.”

Ezekiel 37:19

L'ets Echad is a Torah teaching ministry rooted in Ezekiel 37:19, where the stick of Judah and the stick of Ephraim become one in the hand of HaShem. The name means “into one stick.” We teach from the Tanakh first and meet people where they are, whatever their background or starting point. One focus drives everything. Return.

Return to HaShem, Return to Torah, Return to the Land, Return to the Covenant.
