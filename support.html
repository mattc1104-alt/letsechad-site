Why Give

Invest in the Work of Reunification

L'ets Echad is a teaching ministry. Every dvar Torah, every study guide, every resource is freely available. There is no paywall, no premium tier. The mission is to make Torah teaching accessible to anyone sensing the call of return — regardless of where they are financially.

Your support makes that possible. It funds new teaching preparation, study materials, conference participation, and the ongoing work of getting this content in front of people who need it.

We believe that Ephraim's return is a prophetic work — and that work is happening now. Every gift is an investment in what Hashem is doing in this generation.

Your gift supports

→ Teaching preparation and research for monthly drashot

→ Written study guides and downloadable resources

→ Conference teaching participation

→ Website, hosting, and content infrastructure
